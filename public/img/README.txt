PUT PICTURES IN THIS FOLDER (public/img)

After uploading a picture here, refer to it with /img/ in front of the file name.
Example: you upload about-banner.jpg, then in src/content/site.js write:   about: "/img/about-banner.jpg",
Use .jpg or .webp, ideally under 500 KB each (wide banner pictures about 2000 pixels across; photos about 1200 pixels).
The full list of picture spots is in README.md.
